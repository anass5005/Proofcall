Frequently asked questions

What exactly is ProofSold?

ProofSold is a planned application that helps businesses verify delivery contact attempts and review delivery attempt records when a delivery is marked unsuccessful.

Who is ProofSold designed for?

Businesses that send products to customers through delivery drivers and want better evidence when a delivery is reported as failed.

How does delivery verification work?

ProofSold is designed to record delivery assignments and help establish whether the required customer contact attempt occurred. The exact methods are still being developed.

Does ProofSold replace delivery companies?

No. It's intended to work alongside your delivery operations by adding accountability, not to deliver goods itself.

Is ProofSold available yet?

Not yet. ProofSold is currently in development, and features on this site are planned rather than live.
