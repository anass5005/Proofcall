FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

A quick introduction to ProofCall.

What is ProofCall?

ProofCall is a delivery confirmation app concept designed to help make delivery handoffs and confirmation records clearer.

Who is it designed for?

It is intended for people and teams who need a clearer way to confirm deliveries. Availability and user access may depend on the product release.

How does delivery confirmation work?

The intended workflow is to start a delivery, record the handoff, and use the confirmation as a reference. The exact supported confirmation method will be explained in the app.

Is ProofCall available now?

Availability and supported platforms will be announced through official ProofCall updates.

How is my information handled?
